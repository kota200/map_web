kallisto-web v9 FASTQ fix

Changes from v8:
1. Corrected malformed bundled test FASTQ quality strings. In v8, quality lengths were 35 for reads whose sequence lengths were 32-34.
2. Added browser-side FASTQ structural preflight for the first few records (including best-effort gzip inspection where DecompressionStream is available).
3. Added WORKERFS mounted-file size diagnostics before kallisto starts.
4. Added a specific diagnostic when kallisto reports "processed 0 reads".

No change to kallisto/Bifrost WebAssembly compilation is required for this fix.
